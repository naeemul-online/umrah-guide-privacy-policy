Introduction & Overview

Umrah Guide: Every Step Tracker ("the App", "we", "our") is designed as a focused, respectful, and privacy-first digital companion for pilgrims performing Umrah. We believe that spiritual journeys require peace of mind and complete digital privacy.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding information collection, storage, and user privacy in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play User Data Policies.

Key Summary: The App does not collect, store, transmit, monetize, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or device data. All guidance, counters, checklists, and audio files operate locally on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information whatsoever when you download, install, or use the App:

  • No Account Creation: You do not need to register, provide an email address, phone number, name, or password.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or any third-party tracking or diagnostic SDKs.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect or request the Google Advertising ID (AAID) or hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address).
  • No Network Transmission: The App does not connect to external servers, APIs, or cloud backends. All Dua datasets, transliterations, translations, checklists, and audio clips are bundled directly inside the local application binary.

2. Local Device Storage & Persistence

To ensure a seamless user experience, the App stores lightweight user progress locally on your device using standard mobile storage (React Native AsyncStorage). This local data includes:

  • Checklist item toggles (packed / unpacked preparation items).
  • Current journey stage (Preparation, Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, Halq/Taqsir).
  • Interactive Tawaf round counts (0 to 7) and Sa'i lap counts (0 to 7).
  • User interface preferences (selected language: English, Bengali, or Arabic; selected font size).

Data Ownership & Reset: This data remains strictly in your device's private sandboxed storage space and is never synchronized with any remote server. You can permanently wipe this data at any time by tapping "Reset Journey Data" in the Settings screen, or by clearing app storage / uninstalling the app via your device settings.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

The App declares and requests zero sensitive Android permissions in its manifest:

  • No Location access (GPS or network coarse location).
  • No Camera or Photo Library access.
  • No Microphone or Audio Recording access.
  • No Contacts, SMS, or Phone State access.
  • No External Storage access.

4. Third-Party Services and Libraries

The App is built with React Native and Expo. It contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, social media plugins, or monetization partners. All open-source packages utilized are strictly for client-side rendering, navigation, and local audio playback (expo-av).

5. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The App does not knowingly collect, request, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Economic Area). Because the App collects no user data whatsoever, it is fully safe for individuals and families of all ages.

6. Security and Integrity

Because we do not transmit or host your data on external servers, your information is protected by your device's native operating system security sandbox. We recommend keeping your Android OS updated and utilizing standard device passcode / biometric lock protection.

7. Google Play Data Safety Declaration Summary

For transparency with the Google Play Store Data Safety section:

  • Data Collected: None.
  • Data Shared with Third Parties: None.
  • Data Encrypted in Transit: N/A (no network transit).
  • Account Deletion Mechanism: N/A (no accounts exist; user can reset all local data at any time within the app).

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future app enhancements or regulatory policy updates. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Modified" timestamp.
